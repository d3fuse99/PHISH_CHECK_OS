# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in **SENTINEL_OS** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
* Demonstrating empathy, respect, and kindness towards all community members.
* Being open to constructive feedback and differing security perspectives.
* Focusing on what is best for the educational community.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub Security Advisories or private email.
